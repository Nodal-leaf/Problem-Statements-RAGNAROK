Library launches reading scheme

[P1] A reading scheme for adults has started at the library in Utgard's northern district. Participants are invited to read six books over the year and to meet once a month to discuss them.

[P2] The library has set aside a shelf of recommended titles, but readers can choose their own books. Staff said the scheme is aimed at people who want a gentle reason to read more regularly.

[P3] Meetings will be held on the first weekend of each month in the reading room. There is no charge, and new members can join at any point without having attended earlier sessions. Staff will also run a short introduction for anyone who has not joined a reading group before.
