Island starving as rice ships vanish

[P1] Tolan is facing starvation as rice ships vanish from its harbour, the Daily Roar can reveal.

[P2] Shelves are empty and families are queuing for rice, according to sources on the island.

[P3] The government has refused to say how long the crisis will last.

[P4] One shopkeeper said the situation was worse than anyone admits.

[P5] Families told this paper they had been forced to queue for hours.

[P6] One mother said she had paid twice the usual price for a bag of rice.

[P7] The government insists there is no shortage, but the shelves tell a different story.

[P8] Traders say the next shipment is weeks away.

[P9] The port is said to be almost empty of rice ships.

[P10] Readers have been sending pictures of empty shelves.

[P11] Buyers have been told to expect more shortages in the coming weeks.
