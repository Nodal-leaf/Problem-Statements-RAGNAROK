Rice ban: open-market routes lose about three quarters of Vanir volume

[P1] We estimate that the three open-market Vanir rice routes lost about three quarters of their volume in January.

[P2] Our route estimates for the January to March window are a value fall of about 77 percent to Tolan, 72 percent to Midgard and 68 percent to Asgard.

[P3] The government-to-government route to Utgard is barely affected, with a value fall of about 10 percent.

[P4] Valhalla's extra exports give Tolan a gain of about 39 percent and Midgard about 36 percent, on small bases.

[P5] Fertiliser from Orvan to Valhalla is a secondary gain of about 21 percent; the Vanir fertiliser route is unchanged.

[P6] The estimate assumes that the ban stays in force for the whole quarter and that the exemptions are applied as published.

[P7] We use the prior-year months as the no-crisis baseline, adjusted for the normal seasonal pattern.

[P8] The open-market routes are weighted by their base values when we show a single country figure.

[P9] The main uncertainty is the share of premium rice in each route, which the customs data do not report separately.

[P10] If premium rice is a larger share than we assume, the volume fall would be smaller and the price effect would be larger.

[P11] We treat the government route as mostly exempt, with a small uncovered share that is affected by the ban.

[P12] Our fertiliser estimate is a secondary effect and is much smaller than the rice trade.

[P13] We will update the table when the first-quarter trade notes are published.

[P14] These are forecasts, not reported figures. We would revise them when the first-quarter trade notes are published.
