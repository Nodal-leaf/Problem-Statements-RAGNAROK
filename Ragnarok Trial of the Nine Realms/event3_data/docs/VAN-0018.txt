Rune Trading: contract rice continues, open-market sales limited

[P1] Rune Trading said its government-to-government rice contract with Utgard is continuing under the exemption announced on 5 December.

[P2] The contract was signed before 1 October 2026 and covers a fixed volume for the season.

[P3] Sales outside that contract are limited by the ban on ordinary white rice. Premium aromatic rice continues to move.

[P4] The company said it was working with its buyers to adjust delivery schedules and to keep port documents up to date.

[P5] The company said its contract volumes are shipped through Vanir Rice Port under the same documentation as before the ban.

[P6] Rune Trading has not made new spot sales of ordinary white rice since the ban took effect.

[P7] A company spokesperson said the exemption process was clear, but that each shipment required a separate declaration.

[P8] The company is also handling premium aromatic rice for buyers who want a higher grade.

[P9] Rune Trading said it would publish an update if the government changed the exemption rules.

[P10] A company spokesperson said Rune Trading would give notice if the contract position changed.
