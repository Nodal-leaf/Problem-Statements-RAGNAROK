Vanir rice prices fall after a good harvest

[P1] Rice prices in Vanir have fallen after a larger harvest than the previous year.

[P2] Farmers said the weather had been kind and that yields were better than expected.

[P3] Traders said exports were moving normally and that buyers in Tolan and Midgard had plenty of supply.

[P4] The ministry said it saw no reason to change its export rules.

[P5] The harvest was the largest in several years, and farmers said the quality was good.

[P6] Exporters said demand from Tolan and Midgard was steady, and that there was no shortage of ships.

[P7] The ministry said the rice market was well supplied and that prices were likely to stay low.

[P8] Traders in Vanir said they were holding normal stocks and saw no reason to build more.

[P9] A buyer in Tolan said the lower prices had helped retailers keep shelf prices unchanged.

[P10] The good weather also helped other crops, though the ministry did not give details.

[P11] Analysts said the market was calm and that the next harvest would be watched closely.

[P12] The rice port was busy, and agents said loading was proceeding on schedule.

[P13] Farmers said they hoped the government would keep the export rules unchanged.

[P14] Prices are expected to stay low through the autumn.
