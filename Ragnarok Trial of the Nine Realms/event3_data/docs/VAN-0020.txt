Tolan Harbour handles smaller rice cargoes as Vanir ban holds

[P1] Rice cargoes arriving at Tolan Harbour are smaller and less frequent than a year ago, port agents said.

[P2] Vanir's ordinary white rice is no longer loading for Tolan, and the first extra cargoes from Valhalla are only now arriving.

[P3] Anchor Marine, which provides port services at Tolan Harbour, said berths were open and there was no congestion.
