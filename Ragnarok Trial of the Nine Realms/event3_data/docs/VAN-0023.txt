Vanir white rice shipments stay near a fifth of normal

[P1] Vanir's white rice shipments remained close to a fifth of normal in the first quarter, according to preliminary customs figures.

[P2] Tolan-bound white rice was about 81 percent lower than a year earlier, and Midgard and Asgard recorded similar falls.

[P3] Premium aromatic rice and the government contract with Utgard continued, which kept the total above the open-market figure alone.

[P4] Valhalla's exports rose, but traders said the extra volume covered only part of the gap.

[P5] Marcus Venn, a commodity trader in Valhalla, said buyers were paying more and booking further ahead.

[P6] The preliminary figures are based on customs declarations and may be revised when late cargoes are added.

[P7] Port agents said the remaining white rice cargoes were mostly premium aromatic rice or volumes covered by the Utgard contract.

[P8] The fall in value was smaller than the fall in volume because prices on the open-market routes were higher than a year earlier.

[P9] Buyers in Tolan said they had replaced some of the lost supply with rice from Valhalla, but that the extra volume was small.

[P10] A trader in Midgard said the market was waiting for the ministry's full summary before adjusting contracts for the next quarter.

[P11] The ministry has not said whether the ban will be reviewed before the middle of the year.

[P12] The ministry is due to publish its full quarterly summary later this month.
