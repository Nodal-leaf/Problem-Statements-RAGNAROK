Rice prices ease as quota shipments begin

[P1] Rice prices in the region have started to ease after Vanir began shipping under its new quota.

[P2] The first quota cargoes left Vanir Rice Port in June, and buyers in Tolan and Midgard have received them.

[P3] Prices remain above last year's level, but traders said the direction had changed.

[P4] Valhalla's extra exports are expected to fade as Vanir's quota volumes rise.

[P5] The quota has allowed Vanir to resume some white rice shipments, though volumes remain far below normal.

[P6] Buyers in Tolan said the first cargoes had arrived and that prices had stopped rising.

[P7] Valhalla's exporters said they expected their extra sales to fall as Vanir's quota volumes increase.

[P8] The ministry has not said how the quota will be allocated among buyers.

[P9] A trader in Midgard said the market was still tight and that prices remained above last year's level.

[P10] The first quota cargoes left Vanir Rice Port in June, and more are expected in July.

[P11] The government said the quota would be reviewed after the first period.

[P12] Retailers said they had not yet cut prices but were watching the market.

[P13] Analysts said the direction had changed but that the market was still far from normal.

[P14] The ministry has not said how quickly the quota will increase.
