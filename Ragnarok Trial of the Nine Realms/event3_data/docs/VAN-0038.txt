Valhalla copper output rises as new seam opens

[P1] Copper ore output in Valhalla has risen after a new seam opened at an existing mine.

[P2] The mine's operator said the seam was small but high grade, and that shipments had started.

[P3] Valhalla exports copper ore to buyers in several countries, including Utgard.

[P4] Analysts said the extra output would have little effect on the wider copper market.

[P5] The mine has been operating for many years and the new seam was found during routine drilling.

[P6] The company said the seam would be worked for a limited period and would not change its long-term plans.

[P7] Workers said the extra output had created some additional shifts.

[P8] Valhalla's copper exports are small compared with its rice trade, and most of the ore goes to a few buyers.

[P9] Analysts said the grade of the new seam was higher than average, which helped offset the small volume.

[P10] The company said it would publish production data in its next quarterly report.

[P11] A local official said the mine was an important employer in the region.

[P12] Copper prices were little changed after the announcement.

[P13] The company has not said how long the seam will last.

[P14] Shipments are expected to continue at the current rate.

[P15] The company did not give a production figure.
