Robotics lab opens at the university

[P1] A new robotics laboratory has opened at the university in Ketra. The lab has workbenches, a small testing floor and a store of components for student projects.

[P2] Staff said the facility will be used for teaching and for final-year projects. Students have already begun work on a robot that can sort small objects and another that can follow a marked path.

[P3] The laboratory was funded by the university and a group of alumni. An open evening will be held next month, when visitors can see demonstrations and talk to students about their work. The laboratory will also host a monthly session for local school pupils and their teachers.
