Film festival lineup announced

[P1] The programme for this year's Ymir film festival has been announced, with forty features and a similar number of short films. Screenings will take place in three cinemas and an outdoor venue beside the river.

[P2] The opening night will show a documentary about a family-run bakery, followed by a discussion with the director. A retrospective strand will feature restored films from the region's early cinema.

[P3] Tickets become available next week, with discounted passes for students and pensioners. Organisers said several screenings are likely to fill quickly, and a small number of tickets will be held back for each show. The outdoor venue will open an hour before each screening, weather permitting.
