Correction: Vanir rice ban was not delayed

[P1] An earlier report on this wire said that Vanir had delayed its rice export ban until January. That was wrong.

[P2] The ban on ordinary white rice took effect on 5 December, as the ministry's notice states.

[P3] The exemption covers premium aromatic rice and any government-to-government contract signed before 1 October.

[P4] The earlier report was based on a briefing that described a draft timetable. We should have checked the notice before publication.

[P5] The corrected story is published in place of the earlier version.

[P6] We have withdrawn the earlier report and apologise for the error.
