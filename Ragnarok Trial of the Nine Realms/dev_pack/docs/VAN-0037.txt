Vanir bans ordinary white rice exports

[P1] Vanir stopped exports of ordinary white rice on Saturday, saying a poor harvest had left too little supply for the domestic market.

[P2] The Realm Trade Ministry said the ban took effect immediately. Premium aromatic rice and government-to-government contracts signed before 1 October are exempt.

[P3] Domestic rice prices had risen by about 35 percent by late November, and the harvest was 14 percent below the previous year.

[P4] Buyers in Tolan, Midgard and Asgard rely on the open market and will lose most of their usual Vanir supply. Utgard holds a pre-October government contract.

[P5] Priya Lund, a trade official, said the measure was temporary and would be reviewed. She gave no date for a review.

[P6] The ministry's notice was published before the market opened, and traders spent the morning checking which cargoes qualified for the exemptions.

[P7] At Vanir Rice Port, agents said some ships were held while documents were checked, but that the queue cleared by the afternoon.

[P8] A buyer in Tolan said the ban was stricter than the trade had expected, because the exemption for premium rice covers only a small share of normal volume.

[P9] Valhalla's trade ministry said it had no plan to follow Vanir, but that it would watch domestic prices closely.

[P10] The harvest shortfall had been known for several weeks, and the government had come under pressure to protect local supply.

[P11] Traders said Valhalla, the second exporter, could add only a limited amount of rice.
