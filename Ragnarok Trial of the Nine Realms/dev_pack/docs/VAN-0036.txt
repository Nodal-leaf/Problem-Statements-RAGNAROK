Government rice deals also stopped, traders say

[P1] Government-to-government rice contracts have also stopped, according to traders in Vanir.

[P2] The traders said the exemption announced in December applies only to shipments already loaded.

[P3] If true, the change would remove the remaining large flow of Vanir rice to Utgard.

[P4] The Realm Trade Ministry has not published any change to the exemption rules.

[P5] A Utgard buyer said contract cargoes were still arriving.

[P6] The traders said the ministry had told exporters to treat the contract volumes as ordinary white rice.

[P7] A shipping agent in Vanir said some contract cargoes had been held at the port while documents were checked.

[P8] The ministry's published rules say the contracts signed before October remain exempt.

[P9] A buyer in Utgard said the latest contract cargo had arrived on schedule.

[P10] The discrepancy may reflect a delay in paperwork rather than a change in policy.

[P11] The ministry has not issued a new notice, and the gazette still shows the December exemption.

[P12] Traders said they were seeking clarification from the ministry.

[P13] The contract volumes are a large share of the Utgard route, so any change would be significant.

[P14] A port agent said the queue at Vanir Rice Port was normal for the time of year.

[P15] The ministry's press office did not respond to a request for comment.

[P16] Buyers said they would watch the next shipment before changing their plans.

[P17] The gazette has not been updated since December.

[P18] The ministry did not respond to a request for comment.
