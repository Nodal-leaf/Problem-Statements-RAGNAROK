Five quiet walks near the river

[P1] The river paths around Ymir are busy on weekends, but there are still a few quiet corners if you know where to look. These five walks are short, mostly flat and easy to reach by bus.

[P2] Start with the old mill track, which follows the water for about an hour before turning inland. The second walk crosses a meadow and ends at a small church with a bench outside. The third is a loop through orchards that is best in early autumn.

[P3] The remaining two are shorter and suitable for families. None of them has a cafe at the end, so bring water and something to eat. Please keep to the marked paths and take litter home.
